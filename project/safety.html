Safety First Protocol

LPG Handling & Emergency Guidelines

Liquefied Petroleum Gas is clean, powerful, and safe when handled correctly. Review our essential cylinder management and auto-gas refueling rules.

Everyday LPG Safety Rules

Follow these mandatory guidelines at home, in commercial kitchens, and at our refilling pumps.

1. Storage & Position

Always keep cylinders upright on a flat, level surface in an open, well-ventilated space. Never store cylinders in basements, unventilated cupboards, or near open heat sources.

2. The Soapy Water Test

Never check for gas leaks with a naked flame or match. Apply a soapy water solution to the cylinder valve, hose joints, and regulator. If growing bubbles appear, you have a leak.

3. Vehicle Autogas Refueling

Always turn off your vehicle engine before refueling begins. Passengers must exit the car, mobile phones must remain off, and refueling nozzles must only be operated by certified pump attendants.

What to Do If You Smell Gas (Odorant Detected)

  • Do NOT strike matches, ignite lighters, or turn electrical switches ON or OFF.
  • Extinguish all open flames immediately.
  • Turn off the cylinder regulator valve clockwise.
  • Open doors and windows to promote natural airflow.
  • Evacuate the premises and contact emergency services or your nearest PKAB Gas branch.

Routine Equipment Inspection Checklist

  • Inspect rubber hoses for cracks, stiffness, or abrasions every 6 months.
  • Replace rubber gas hoses every 2 to 3 years.
  • Check that the regulator rubber O-ring seal is elastic and undamaged before refilling.
  • Never use worn or dented cylinders past their mandatory hydrostatic test dates.
